# Case 4: SportsInsight

*Case for academic purposes. The company and its figures are illustrative.*

## Case description

SportsInsight is a niche sports news portal (advanced statistics and analysis). Its business model consists of attracting a large audience with free content and then converting those users into paying subscribers of a premium analysis service. For the model to work, it needs high loyalty and recurrence.

The portal has a large volume of traffic and a good number of new users, but the proportion of returning users is low and the average interval between one visit and the next exceeds 15 days. In essence, users visit the site, consume one article and do not come back.

The company has invested in a push notification system and in daily newsletters, but engagement remains low. Management's main fear is that, if retention does not increase, the user base will not be loyal enough to consider the premium subscription.

## Current context

The technical team detects in the server logs a growing volume of requests from AI crawlers on the statistics pages, while the traffic referred from those same assistants is small. Management wonders whether its free content is being consumed through AI answers instead of on the portal, and what decision to take about it.

## Web analytics challenge

The analytical challenge focuses on retention and loyalty metrics. It must be identified which type of content or which dimension of interaction creates a bond with the user and which one favors their churn, also considering the effect of AI assistants on content consumption.

3. **Critical dimensions:** Which user and content dimensions are crucial to understand the low loyalty?
4. **Analytical strategy:** How would you explain, and what strategy would you use, to identify which content generates the most engagement and encourages recurrence?
5. **A/B test:** Propose an A/B test hypothesis to increase recurrence or user registration. State the primary metric, the guardrail metrics and the decision criterion, and justify its potential impact.
6. **AI and agents:** Which indicators would you use to evaluate the relationship between AI crawler activity and the visits they generate (for example, the crawl-to-refer ratio)? What decisions could the company make with that information?
